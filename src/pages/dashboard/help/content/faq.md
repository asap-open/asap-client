# FAQ & Quick Tips

Answers to common questions about using ASAP.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does ASAP save my active workout if I close the app?
Yes. Active workouts are automatically preserved in local session storage. When you return to the app or reopen the session logger, your logged sets, reps, and exercises are restored.

### How is 1RM (One Rep Max) calculated?
ASAP calculates estimated 1RM using standard formulas based on the weight lifted and repetitions completed on hard sets.

### How often should I log my weight?
We recommend weighing in under consistent morning conditions (after waking up, before breakfast) 3 to 7 times per week for accurate trend tracking.
