# Home Dashboard

The **Home** tab is your central hub for tracking daily metrics and reviewing recent workouts.

## Key Features

* **Daily Status:** Displays current day greeting and athlete summary.
* **Log Weight:** Quick modal to record your daily bodyweight. Entries update your bodyweight history charts automatically.
* **Recent Activity Feed:** Chronological list of your recent training sessions with volume, set counts, and personal record badges.
* **Session Actions:** Tap any past workout to view details, edit exercises, duplicate as a new workout, or delete.
