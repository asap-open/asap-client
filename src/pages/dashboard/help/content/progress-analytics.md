# Progress & Analytics

ASAP turns your training data into actionable sports-science insights, helping you optimize volume and maintain balanced muscle development.

## Dashboard Breakdown

* **Interactive Calendar Heatmap:** View workout frequency, session density, and total tonnage over time. Click any day to view complete workout logs.
* **Key Performance Indicators:** Track Total Volume (kg lifted), Session Count, Total Sets, and Average Intensity across selected time windows.
* **Muscle Balance Radar:** Check volume distribution (Push, Pull, Legs, Core) against your chosen Training Mode split targets.
* **1RM & PB Timeline:** Track strength gains and personal records across key compound movements.
* **Bodyweight & BMI Trends:** Monitor body composition trends over weekly and monthly windows.
