# Routines & Settings

Configure workout templates and personalize your ASAP experience.

## Settings Sections

* **Appearance & Theme:** Switch between Light, Dark, or System mode to match your preferences.
* **Manage Routines:** Build and organize saved workout templates with predefined exercises, sets, and rep ranges.
* **Tracked Exercises:** Select benchmark lifts to highlight prominently in progress charts and 1RM tracking.
