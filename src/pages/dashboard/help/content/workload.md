# Workload Volume & Safety

ASAP tracks physical training demand by measuring **Volume** (Total Weight Lifted × Reps) using sports-science metrics to keep you progressing safely.

## ACWR (Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio)
This metric compares your immediate fatigue to your historical fitness level for injury prevention.

- **Acute Load:** Your total volume over the **last 7 days**.
- **Chronic Load:** Your average weekly volume over the **previous 4 weeks**.
- **The Ratio:** `Acute Load / Chronic Load`

### How to read your ACWR Status:
* **Optimal (0.8 – 1.3):** The target zone. You are training enough to progress without excessive injury risk.
* **High (1.3 – 1.5):** Elevated load. Pay attention to recovery and fatigue signals.
* **Danger (> 1.5):** Spiked training volume too quickly compared to baseline. High injury risk.
* **Under (< 0.8):** Training below baseline. If continued long term, this leads to detraining.

## Ramp Rate
Measures your immediate progression rate between consecutive weeks.
- **Calculation:** `Current 7-Day Volume - Previous 7-Day Volume`
- **Purpose:** Highlights sudden jumps in weekly tonnage.
